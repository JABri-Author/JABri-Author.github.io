Moonlit Embers

Adult epic fantasy

When a tryst becomes a quest to restore a fractured deity, Eanys and Alysande find their faith tested, not least of all in themselves.

A story of love, acceptance, the monsters we make, and those we are made to become.

Read the samples

Roughly 133,000 words across 25 chapters. Dual protagonists, six POV characters. Complete and revised.

Contains material intended for adult readers, including slavery, sexual coercion, and depictions of violence and its aftermath. A full list of content notes is below.

Content Warning

Please read before proceeding


This is adult fantasy and it goes to some dark places. Reading it is entirely voluntary and stopping partway is completely fine—just tell me where and why, that's useful data.

The book contains:

• Sexual content between adults, nothing explicit, fade to black

• Slavery, slave markets, and characters bought and sold

• A state-run programme of forced reproduction.

• Conscription of children, established indirectly rather than depicted

• Threatened sexual assault depicted twice, briefly, aimed at protagonists by groups of armed men, neither goes further than the threat; and one separate incident of unwanted groping by a stranger, early.

• Dead children exist but no child dies on the page, but children are found dead and grieved over more than once

• Torture, immolation, burning alive, and detailed combat gore

• Self Destruction where a character sells herself into slavery

• Body horror such as reanimated corpses, transformation, dismemberment

If any of that is a hard no, say so and I'll find another reader. No explanation needed and no hard feelings.
